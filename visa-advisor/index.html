Before you begin

This tool provides general information based on publicly available sources as of the date shown on each report. It is:

  • Not legal advice.
  • Not a substitute for an immigration attorney.
  • Subject to rules that change frequently.

Visa requirements are determined solely by the destination country's authorities. Always verify with the destination embassy or consulate before booking travel.

For comprehensively sanctioned destinations (Iran, North Korea, Syria, Crimea / DNR / LNR), this tool does not provide visa guidance — please consult a licensed immigration attorney.
